## v0.9.212

- Fix broken SMTP brand logos by converting supported HTTP(S) and data-URL images, including SVG and WebP, into embedded PNG attachments.
- Bound logo downloads and image dimensions, and cache converted artwork. Unreadable or unsupported logos fall back to the styled brand name without blocking delivery.
